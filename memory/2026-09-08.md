## Warm human media voice directive and complete audit

- Wyatt clarified that removing explicit placeholders was not enough. Captions that are accurate but clinical, formulaic, over-explained or emotionally distant still fail the focusChrist standard.
- Permanent voice: calm, sincere, reverent and human. Begin with the exact visible or audible moment, let its real emotion emerge naturally, and connect it to Christ or the nearby study without abstract AI bridge language. Do not invent facts, relationships, emotions or source claims in pursuit of warmth.
- The complete public HTML inventory was audited: 35 pages, 127 captions, 92 expanded-detail paragraphs and 75 resource cards. Rewrote the weak language across Answer studies, the Answers hub, Art, Church History, General Conference, Mission and Pioneers, including the four owner-supplied examples from the D&C 6, Joseph Smith and divorce studies.
- Repeated distant constructions such as “reflects the,” “represents,” “embodies,” “makes room for,” “allowing uncertainty,” “directing attention,” “holding space” and disclaimer-like qualifications were removed from media descriptions where they substituted explanation for feeling.
- Updated every duplicated artwork placement ledger that carried the rewritten descriptions so hidden records cannot restore stale wording.
- Added `tools/media_voice_qa.py` to pull-request QA and the GitHub Pages deployment gate. Future releases fail if the audited media inventory shrinks unexpectedly or clinical placeholder patterns return in captions and expanded artwork copy.
