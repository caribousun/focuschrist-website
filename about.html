Why this site exists

About focusChrist

focusChrist is an independent Christ-centered study resource created to help sincere visitors explore Jesus Christ, scripture, Latter-day Saint belief, faithful history, and trustworthy paths for continued study.

Our purpose

Help people turn toward Jesus Christ

focusChrist exists to make sincere study easier: ask a question, explore scripture, follow sources, learn from faithful history, and continue into authoritative Church resources when deeper verification is needed.

What we believe

Jesus Christ is the center

Content is presented from a Latter-day Saint perspective that worships Jesus Christ as Savior and Redeemer, accepts His Atonement and Resurrection, and believes His gospel and priesthood authority were restored.

“For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son...” — John 3:16

How the site works

Study with clarity and source awareness

Ask

The Q&A experience combines curated material with AI-assisted responses when needed. Conversation is designed to support study, not replace scripture or official Church teaching.

Verify

Scripture and official Church resources are linked whenever available. AI-assisted responses are study aids and may contain errors; they should be checked against authoritative sources for doctrine, policy, and history.

Continue

Permanent Answers, Art & Study, Pioneer history, Watch & Study, Gospel Library, and other official resources provide natural next steps after a question is answered.

What you can expect from a study here

focusChrist offers a Latter-day Saint perspective while welcoming readers who believe differently or are still deciding what they believe. Clear explanations should help you understand a teaching without asking you to pretend that differences or difficult questions do not exist.

Scripture links take you to the passages being discussed. Official Church resources explain Church teachings and practices. Historical records support claims about the past. Original reflection offers a way to think or act; artwork offers a visual interpretation. Those purposes overlap, but they are not interchangeable kinds of evidence.

See a respectful comparison in practice

Explore shared Christian commitments and real doctrinal differences, with sources for the traditions being described.

See how a historical question is studied

Compare the setting, date, and purpose of accounts before drawing a conclusion.

See scripture, image, and reflection work together

Read John 10 and Psalm 23, distinguish the artist’s choices from the text, and consider a personal application.

You remain free to question an explanation, check the source, or leave a matter unresolved. The purpose of these connections is to make careful learning easier and to treat the person doing that learning with respect.
