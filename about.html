Why this site exists

About focusChrist

focusChrist is an independent Christ-centered study resource created to help sincere visitors explore Jesus Christ, scripture, Latter-day Saint belief, faithful history, and trustworthy paths for continued study.

Our purpose

Help people turn toward Jesus Christ

focusChrist exists to make sincere study easier: ask a question, explore scripture, follow sources, learn from faithful history, and continue into authoritative Church resources when deeper verification is needed.

What we believe

Jesus Christ is the center

Content is presented from a Latter-day Saint perspective that worships Jesus Christ as Savior and Redeemer, accepts His Atonement and Resurrection, and believes His gospel and priesthood authority were restored.

“For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son...” — John 3:16

How the site works

Study with clarity and source awareness

Ask

The Q&A experience combines curated material with AI-assisted responses when needed. Conversation is designed to support study, not replace scripture or official Church teaching.

Verify

Scripture and official Church resources are linked whenever available. AI-assisted responses can contain errors and should be checked against authoritative sources for doctrine, policy, and history.

Continue

Permanent Answers, Art & Study, Pioneer history, Watch & Study, Gospel Library, and other official resources provide natural next steps after a question is answered.
