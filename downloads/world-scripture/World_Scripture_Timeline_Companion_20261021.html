A companion to the World Scripture Timeline

Walking the Timeline

This companion walks beside the timeline, entry by entry, from the first settlements after the ice to the last cities of the medieval Americas. It tells you, in plain words, what each entry is, when it belongs, where it happened, and what the date rests on, so you can read the whole sweep as one long journey.

Nothing here is in a hurry. Read it straight through, or wander. Every place name that can be opened on a map is a link, and the peoples are named as they appear, the way you would meet them on the road.

How to read this companion

Three records run side by side. World entries carry the colors of the earth's peoples and rest on the published sources named under each one. Bible entries follow the dates scholars commonly cite, and the earliest ones the traditional chronology. Book of Mormon entries are dated where the record itself states the year, or one plain step of arithmetic from a count the record states, and the verse behind the year is printed with the entry. A few of the earliest entries, the Jaredite ones, are the exception: the book of Ether gives no year count, so those years are traditional approximate placements, and each of those entries says so in its own note.

The Book of Mormon's promised land is shown two ways on the timeline, in a Mesoamerica setting and in a North America setting, because the record's own internal map is what the timeline asks you to weigh. Promised land entries therefore carry two map links, one for each setting. Places the record anchors in the old world, like Jerusalem, carry one.

Where a people is known, the entry wears their name. On the timeline itself, the People selector above the map can follow any one of them across the whole journey.

Nsude Pyramid Shrines World

Date not established · Nigeria

Ten Igbo mud shrine pyramids at Nsude, Enugu State, Nigeria. NO reliable archaeological date exists: they were documented ethnographically in the early 1900s (photographed 1935), and at least one account says the set seen then had been built only two years earlier. The BC dates and the Egypt placement in the original sheet are not supported, so this entry is deliberately left undated.
