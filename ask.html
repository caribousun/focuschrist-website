Ask, seek, and knock A Question Can Begin a Study Journey

Jesus invited people to ask, seek, and knock. The open scriptures and quiet conversation connect that invitation with sincere questions, prayer, and a willingness to keep learning.

The open book and quiet conversation show that faithful questions need not lead away from God. They can lead into scripture, prayer, patient learning, and deeper trust in Jesus Christ.

A question brought to Christ Jesus Christ and Nicodemus

John 3 records Nicodemus coming to Jesus by night and asking questions. Their conversation includes the Savior's teaching about being born again and God's love in sending His Son.

Nicodemus approached the Savior with questions and listened as Jesus taught of spiritual rebirth, faith, and eternal life. Their quiet exchange reminds seekers that coming to Christ begins with humility and a willingness to receive truth.

Understanding can unfold He Opened the Scriptures

On the road to Emmaus, two disciples spoke with the risen Lord without recognizing Him at first. Jesus listened to their confusion and then taught them from the scriptures.

Their journey shows that questions, patient study, and the Savior's teaching can turn uncertainty into understanding. As He opened the scriptures, their hearts burned within them and they came to recognize Him.
