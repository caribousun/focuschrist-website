Ask, seek, and knock A Question Can Begin a Study Journey

Jesus invited people to ask, seek, and knock. This symbolic artwork connects that invitation with sincere questions, scripture study, prayer, and a willingness to keep learning.

The scene is a contemporary devotional portrayal, not a documentary image. Christ beside the seeker represents the hope that honest study can draw a person closer to Him.

A question brought to Christ Jesus Christ and Nicodemus

John 3 records Nicodemus coming to Jesus by night and asking questions. Their conversation includes the Savior's teaching about being born again and God's love in sending His Son.

This artwork is a reverent historical portrayal of that conversation. Its setting and expressions help visitors imagine the exchange without claiming to reproduce a documented visual scene.
