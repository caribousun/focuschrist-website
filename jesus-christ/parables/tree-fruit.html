A comparison taught by Jesus Christ

A Tree and Its Fruit

Fruit reveals what an outward appearance can conceal. Jesus connects discernment with conduct, speech, and what the heart cultivates.

Begin the study ↓
Look beyond the outward appearanceWhat a heart brings forthAttend to the fruit you cultivate

Read and discover

Look beyond the outward appearance

Jesus uses a tree's fruit to teach discernment. In Matthew's sermon, the warning concerns false prophets who appear harmless. Fruit provides a way to look beyond the outward covering. The familiar difference between productive trees and thorny growth brings conduct into the question of trust. Matthew 7:15-20

Matthew 7:15-20Matthew 12:33-37Luke 6:43-45

Read and discover

What a heart brings forth

Luke connects fruit with what comes out of the heart. Good and evil treasures issue in corresponding speech and action. Matthew also records the tree comparison in a discussion that includes words and accountability. These related settings should be kept visible rather than collapsed into a single occasion. Luke 6:43-45

The picture asks us to pay attention to what a teaching or way of life produces. It does not make one difficult moment a complete account of a person, nor does it authorize instant certainty about another's heart. Careful discernment can remain patient and open to repentance.

Matthew 7:15-20Matthew 12:33-37Luke 6:43-45

Read and discover

Attend to the fruit you cultivate

For reflection, consider the guidance you receive and the speech you offer. Does it grow honesty, mercy, and faithful action, or deepen contempt and evasion? That question can be brought first to your own habits, where a change in what you cultivate may change what others receive.

Pause and consider

  • What fruit follows the guidance you trust?
  • What does your speech reveal?
