A parable taught by Jesus Christ

The Blind Leading the Blind

A guide cannot supply sight that the guide does not possess. Jesus' brief question asks disciples to consider whom they follow.

Begin the study ↓

Read and discover

A question about the guide

Luke explicitly calls this brief comparison a parable. Jesus asks whether one blind person can lead another and whether both will fall into a ditch. The question appears among teachings about judgment, mercy, and the relationship between a disciple and a teacher. Luke 6:36-42

Luke 6:36-42Matthew 15:13-14

Read and discover

Whom the disciple becomes like

The picture concerns the ability to guide. A confident leader cannot supply sight that the leader does not possess. The following saying about a disciple becoming like a teacher makes the choice of guidance a matter with lasting consequences.

Matthew records a related warning about blind leaders after controversy over human traditions and what defiles a person. Those surrounding passages help explain the spiritual use of the image. Physical blindness itself is not evidence that a person is morally deficient or unable to teach. Matthew 15:13-14

Luke 6:36-42Matthew 15:13-14

Read and discover

Choose guidance with care

Reflection can ask what makes guidance trustworthy and what sort of person a teaching helps you become. Confidence, popularity, and forceful presentation are not the same as sound direction. A disciple may need humility both to seek help and to examine the guidance already being followed.

Pause and consider

  • What makes a guide trustworthy?
  • How does learning from a teacher shape your conduct?
