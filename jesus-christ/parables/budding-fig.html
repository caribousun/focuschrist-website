A parable taught by Jesus Christ

The Budding Fig Tree

New leaves tell an observer something about the season. Jesus invites discernment while leaving the day and hour unknown.

A familiar change of season

Jesus speaks of a fig tree within His discourse about coming events. When its branch becomes tender and leaves appear, observers recognize that summer is near. Luke broadens the view to the fig tree and all the trees. A familiar seasonal change supplies the comparison. Matthew 24:32-44

Recognition without a timetable

The hearers are invited to recognize rather than ignore what is before them. Yet the surrounding discourse also warns that the precise day and hour are unknown. Attentiveness to His words and refusal to calculate an unauthorized timetable belong together. Mark 13:28-31

This is neither the story of a fruitless tree given more time nor the account of Jesus cursing a fig tree. Shared imagery does not make those passages one event. Here the ordinary appearance of leaves illustrates discernment and the reliability of His words. Luke 21:29-33

Readiness in ordinary life

Readiness can become part of ordinary discipleship rather than a cycle of alarm. You might ask what Christ's teaching calls you to do today even without knowing what tomorrow will bring. Caring for others, repenting, and keeping commitments do not depend on discovering a date.

Pause and consider

  • What does recognition require beyond curiosity?
  • How can readiness become daily faithfulness?
