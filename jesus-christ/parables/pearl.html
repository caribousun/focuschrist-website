A parable taught by Jesus Christ

The Pearl of Great Price

A merchant recognizes something worth a wholehearted response. The pearl invites a careful look at seeking, discerning, and choosing.

A merchant who recognizes worth

Jesus places a merchant's search beside the story of treasure found in a field. The merchant is already seeking fine pearls. When he finds one of exceptional worth, he sells all that he has and buys it. The brief account connects discernment with commitment. Matthew 13:45-46

Matthew 13:45-46

Read and discover

Seeking, finding, and choosing

The two stories share a costly response, but their beginnings differ. One person encounters hidden treasure; the other finds what he has been seeking. Reading them together allows room for differing paths toward recognition while preserving the decisive action in each.

The pearl's beauty is not described in detail. Jesus does not give a travel history for the merchant or name the ports he visits. The focus remains on recognizing worth and responding. A visual retelling should keep that concentration rather than invent an adventure around two verses.

Matthew 13:45-46

Read and discover

Giving recognition a response

This picture invites a searching question about priorities: what do you recognize as precious, and how does that recognition affect your choices? Coming to know Christ can deepen both understanding and commitment. The parable offers a way to examine that relationship without comparing the speed of one person's journey with another's.

Pause and consider

  • What distinguishes seeking from recognizing?
  • What commitment follows recognition for you?
