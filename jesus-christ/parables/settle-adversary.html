A comparison taught by Jesus Christ

Settling with an Adversary

There is still an opportunity on the road before judgment. Jesus' comparison invites timely, responsible attention to conflict.

Begin the study ↓

Read and discover

The road toward judgment

In the Sermon on the Mount, Jesus connects anger and reconciliation with a brief picture of two adversaries on their way to judgment. There is an opportunity to address the conflict before they arrive. Delay can carry consequences that are harder to undo. Matthew 5:21-26

Matthew 5:21-26Luke 12:58-59

Read and discover

The opportunity before arrival

Luke records a related saying in teaching about discerning the present time. The path toward the magistrate becomes a setting in which the need for timely action is visible. The picture does not dwell on the full legal dispute or tell us which modern procedure should be followed. Luke 12:58-59

The spiritual invitation concerns taking unresolved wrong seriously while there is an opportunity to respond. It should not be expanded into individualized legal advice or a demand that an injured person negotiate alone with someone unsafe.

Matthew 5:21-26Luke 12:58-59

Take a responsible step toward peace

Reflection might identify a conflict where an honest, responsible step is possible: acknowledging a wrong, correcting an account, or seeking appropriate help. Reconciliation cannot always be secured by one person, but delay need not become an excuse for avoiding the part that is yours.

Pause and consider

  • What conflict needs honest attention?
  • What safe step toward peace is available?
