A parable taught by Jesus Christ

The Fishing Net

The net gathers a mixed catch, and the shore becomes a place of separation. Jesus connects an ordinary fishing scene with the seriousness of judgment.

The whole catch comes ashore

A net cast into the sea gathers fish of every kind. When it is full, the fishers bring it to shore, keep the good in vessels, and discard the bad. Jesus begins with a familiar sequence of work, then explicitly connects the separation with judgment. Matthew 13:47-50

Matthew 13:47-50

Read and discover

Gathering and final separation

The catch is gathered before it is sorted. Those moments belong to one story, but they are not interchangeable. Jesus' explanation identifies angels with the final separation at the end. The hearer is not appointed to determine the final standing of particular people.

The passage has a serious conclusion that should neither be erased nor expanded into imagined scenes of punishment. Its purpose is not satisfied by attractive fishing imagery alone. The account asks readers to consider that the kingdom's gathering and the reality of judgment both matter.

Matthew 13:47-50

Read and discover

Responsibility with humility

A personal response can hold responsibility and humility together. You can examine your own conduct, seek repentance, and treat people with care while acknowledging the limits of your judgment. The Lord's final assessment is not a task that disciples should casually claim for themselves.

Pause and consider

  • How does the shore differ from the gathering stage?
  • What responsibility remains yours without judging another's final outcome?
