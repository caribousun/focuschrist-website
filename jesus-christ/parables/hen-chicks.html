The Hen Gathering Her Chicks

A protective gathering becomes the image of Jesus' lament. The invitation carries love, sorrow, and respect for the hearers' response.

A lament and a protective image

Jesus laments over Jerusalem, speaking of prophets killed and messengers rejected. He describes His desire to gather its children as a hen gathers her chicks beneath her wings. The small, protective image appears within a grief-filled address. Matthew 23:37-39

Matthew 23:37-39Luke 13:34-35

Read and discover

The gathering desired and refused

The gathering is desired but refused. That tension belongs to the passage: His care does not remove the hearers' agency. The image expresses both the shelter He would offer and the sorrow of an invitation that is not received.

Luke preserves the lament in another surrounding sequence of His journey. Reading both accounts can deepen attention to His words without requiring us to resolve every chronological question. Neither account authorizes contempt toward Jewish people today. Luke 13:34-35

Matthew 23:37-39Luke 13:34-35

Offer care while preserving agency

As an application, consider what makes an invitation caring rather than controlling. Protection, belonging, and patient love can be offered while another person's response remains their own. The passage may also invite you to ask what it means to receive the Savior's gathering call personally.

Pause and consider

  • What does gathering show about care?
  • How can you invite while respecting agency?
