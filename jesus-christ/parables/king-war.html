A parable taught by Jesus Christ

The King Considering War

Before advancing, a king weighs what he can meet. Jesus uses the decision to deepen His call to deliberate discipleship.

Another picture of counting the cost

Jesus places a king's decision beside a builder's calculation. Both appear while He is teaching a large accompanying crowd about discipleship. The images ask people to think seriously about the commitment before them rather than move forward only through the momentum of the crowd. Luke 14:25-35

Luke 14:25-35

Read and discover

Assessment before confrontation

The king considers whether ten thousand can meet an opposing force of twenty thousand. If not, he sends a delegation to seek terms of peace while the other king is still far away. The decisive action is thoughtful assessment, not an exciting battle.

The comparison does not report a particular historical campaign or praise aggression. Nor should each army be assigned an unsupplied symbolic identity. Its immediate connection is the seriousness of counting the cost, followed by Jesus' words about forsaking what competes with discipleship.

Luke 14:25-35

Read and discover

The humility to consider

As a reflection, consider where honest assessment might change your response to a commitment. Humility can acknowledge what is beyond your strength. Following Christ may require asking for help and reordering priorities instead of defending the appearance that you already have everything in hand.

Pause and consider

  • What honest assessment are you avoiding?
  • Where would humility change your decision?
