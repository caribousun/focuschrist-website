A parable taught by Jesus Christ

The Faithful and Unfaithful Servant

Someone else's daily bread is placed in a servant's care. The master's absence reveals how that trust will be used.

Begin the study ↓

Responsibility for the household

The master places a servant over the household with responsibility to provide food at the proper time. The question is what that servant will do while the master is absent. Jesus makes readiness concrete through care that other people need to receive. Matthew 24:45-51

Matthew 24:45-51Luke 12:41-48

Read and discover

What the delay reveals

The faithful servant continues the work. The unfaithful servant takes the delay as permission to mistreat others and indulge himself. The master's return reveals which attitude has governed the entrusted authority. Waiting has not been empty time; it has shown the servant's character.

Luke records Peter asking whether the teaching is for the disciples or for everyone. Jesus' answer develops accountability in relation to knowledge and what has been entrusted. Greater responsibility is not merely greater status; it carries an obligation to use the trust faithfully. Luke 12:41-48

Matthew 24:45-51Luke 12:41-48

Read and discover

Care that remains dependable

Consider a place where another person depends on your attention. The parable invites service that remains dependable when no one appears to be checking. Its warning against abuse also matters: authority in the household is given for care, not as a license to dominate.

Pause and consider

  • Who depends on your faithful attention?
  • How does responsibility change when you remember it is entrusted?
