A crowd and the cost of following

Large crowds accompany Jesus when He speaks about the cost of discipleship. The tower appears within that demanding teaching, not as a detached proverb about project planning. Jesus asks His hearers to consider what they are undertaking when they choose to follow Him. Luke 14:25-35

Luke 14:25-35

Read and discover

A foundation is only the beginning

A person intending to build first sits down and counts whether there is enough to finish. If a foundation is laid and the work stops, observers can see the gap between intention and completion. Jesus lets an unfinished structure embody an unfinished commitment.

The companion comparison about a king considering war develops the same call to sober consideration. The passage then returns directly to discipleship. Reading the surrounding verses helps keep the tower from becoming merely advice about avoiding embarrassment.

Luke 14:25-35

Read and discover

Choose with honest commitment

You might ask what support, changed priorities, or sustained effort a faithful commitment will require. Honest consideration is not the same as waiting until nothing will be difficult. It can be part of choosing deliberately, with a clearer understanding that following Christ reaches into the whole life.

Pause and consider

  • What commitment are you considering lightly?
  • What support would help you follow through?
