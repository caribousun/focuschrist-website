A willing heart, a continuing journey

Settling your heart on Christ is a deliberate commitment to follow Him. It can coexist with questions that need patient study. Elder Andersen’s devotional invites listeners to turn their thoughts more often toward the Savior and to embrace the Book of Mormon more fully.

Use the original message as your starting point. The scripture connections below are a focusChrist companion study across the Latter-day Saint canon; they are not a transcript or a claim that Elder Andersen used every pictured story.

Carry one invitation into the week

A seven-day reading practice
  1. Listen to the devotional and choose one invitation.
  2. Read one pictured New Testament account in full.
  3. Compare Ruth’s choice with the three men’s answer in Daniel.
  4. Read the Book of Mormon accounts and notice Christ’s attention to people.
  5. Read Joseph’s account of asking God for wisdom.
  6. Write a remembered experience of guidance, kindness, or peace.
  7. Choose and carry out one act of service. Record what you learned.
Make room for a careful question

Write the question precisely. Separate the passage’s words, a speaker’s testimony, historical evidence, and your own interpretation. Use the linked original sources, speak with someone you trust, and bring the question to God. A sincere question deserves more than a hurried answer.

Remember what strengthened you

What helped you notice the Savior this week? Which passage will you return to? What remains unresolved? Record these honestly; a quiet experience does not need to become a dramatic story to be worth remembering.
