Art and study The Living Christ

This devotional artwork invites reflection on Jesus Christ as the risen and living Son of God. Christian hope centers not only on what He taught, but on the witness that He rose from the tomb and lives.

The gallery title is The Living Christ. The artwork remains unchanged, and focusChrist does not assert artist, date, or provenance beyond the existing gallery record.

Art and study The Good Shepherd

Jesus used the image of a shepherd to describe His relationship with those who follow Him. He knows His sheep, calls them, leads them, protects them, and gives His life for them.

This is devotional artwork connected with that scriptural image. focusChrist does not assert artist, date, or provenance beyond the existing gallery record.

Art and study Suffer the Little Children

When people brought children to Jesus, He welcomed them, took them in His arms, and blessed them. He also used their example to teach humility, trust, and willingness to receive God's kingdom.

This devotional artwork reflects that scriptural account. focusChrist does not assert artist, date, or provenance beyond the existing gallery record.

Art and study Be Still

Psalm 46 places the invitation to be still inside a world of upheaval. Scriptural stillness can mean trusting that God remains God even when circumstances are unsettled.

This devotional artwork invites that reflection. focusChrist does not assert artist, date, or provenance beyond the existing gallery record.
