Begin with the question you are carrying

You do not need to know the vocabulary or have your beliefs settled before you begin. Choose a subject that matters to you, read one passage in its setting, and keep a note of what you understand and what you still want to ask.

I am carrying doubt or fear

Begin with Doctrine and Covenants 6:32-37 and study what it can mean to look unto Christ in every thought.

I am trying to understand prayer

Explore prayer, thoughtful discernment, and choices you can make while a question remains open.

I need hope in a difficult season

Consider strength and support during hardship without measuring faith by how soon circumstances improve.

If images help you slow down, study Be Still with Psalm 46. To hear current prophetic witness, explore the complete April 2026 General Conference study. If your question concerns the historical record, start with a guided comparison of sources.
