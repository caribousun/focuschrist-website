Six paths into the messages

These short introductions lead to full official talks. The reflection questions are optional focusChrist study prompts.

President Henry B. Eyring

Prayer and peace

President Eyring teaches that sincere, continuing prayer invites the Lord’s peace and support through joyful seasons, uncertainty, and grief.

focusChrist reflectionWhat would help me turn to Heavenly Father consistently, including when I do not know what to say?

Prayers for Peace ↗

Sister Kristin M. Yee

Ministering with love

Sister Yee describes ministering as personal, compassionate care patterned after Christ, including choosing to love when we cannot fix another person’s circumstances.

focusChrist reflectionWho could I listen to or care for this week without assuming I must solve everything?

Ministering—“That Ye Love One Another; as I Have Loved You” ↗

Read or watch on the Church website

Elder David A. Bednar

Enduring and becoming

Elder Bednar connects enduring valiantly with receiving charity and becoming more like Christ through His grace, rather than relying on determination alone.

focusChrist reflectionWhere could I seek the Savior’s help to become more loving, beyond simply completing a difficult responsibility?

All Who Have Endured Valiantly ↗

Read or watch on the Church website

President Dieter F. Uchtdorf

Hope in the risen Christ

President Uchtdorf invites us to seek a personal witness of the living Christ and share that witness through courageous, humble discipleship and service.

focusChrist reflectionHow could one humble act of service express my faith in the living Savior today?

Encounter at the Empty Tomb ↗

Read or watch on the Church website

President Dallin H. Oaks

Becoming a peacemaker

President Oaks connects faith in the Resurrection with daily discipleship, urging love for adversaries and peaceful language in family and other relationships.

focusChrist reflectionWhich conversation could I approach with greater respect and fewer harsh words?

Elder Neil L. Andersen

Marriage and Christlike growth

Elder Andersen describes eternal marriage as continuing growth in Christlike qualities, emphasizing covenants, patience, repentance, forgiveness, and reliance on the Holy Ghost.

From listening to living

One message. One meaningful step.

Use this optional practice on your own, with family, or in a class. Give yourself time to understand the message in its setting.

01 · Listen and read

Notice the witness of Christ

Read the full message or watch it on the official page. What does it teach about the Savior’s character, promises, or work?

02 · Follow the scriptures

Study beyond a sentence

Open the cited verses and read their surrounding passages. Write down one question and one connection you want to explore.

03 · Choose an invitation

Make room for action

In your own notebook, finish: “This week, I will…” Choose something specific, then return to the message and reflect on what you learned.
