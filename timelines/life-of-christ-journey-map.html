How to read this map

The first 29 stops follow places in the Holy Land and Egypt. Stops 30 and 31 follow the ministry recorded in 3 Nephi without assigning it a modern geographic location. Seven later entries show where witnesses received appearances and visions. The dotted study sequence stays within the first 29 stops; no route joins the later visions.

  • Individual numbered pins match the list; count markers open nearby events.
  • Use the phase filters to study one part of the life at a time.
  • Full journey shows the mapped places and later witness locations. Choose Americas to study the two Bountiful accounts beside an internal map.
About the Americas location: The Book of Mormon does not give a modern map location for the land Bountiful. These events are not assigned modern geographic coordinates. The Church does not take a position on specific geographic locations in the ancient Americas. Read the Church’s explanation of Book of Mormon geography.

The internal map supplied for this study illustrates relationships among places described in the text. Its shapes, scale and placements are a reconstruction, not an official Church map or an identification of modern lands. Open the complete internal map.

Readers have proposed locations in North, Central and South America. The accompanying Eastern Great Lakes presentation explores one working hypothesis and compares several alternatives, including Mesoamerica. Its conclusions depend on the author’s map and comparison assumptions; they do not establish where the events occurred or rule out other proposals. Eastern Great Lakes comparison: a personal proposal.

Sources and certainty: Scripture references are from the King James Bible, the Book of Mormon, and the Doctrine and Covenants. Read the New Testament, 3 Nephi, and Doctrine and Covenants 19:18 in Gospel Library. Most Holy Land places use standard Bible atlas identifications such as Nazareth, Bethlehem, Capernaum, Bethsaida, Caesarea Philippi, Jericho, and Bethany. A few pins are traditional or representative rather than certain: the Gospels do not name the mountain of the Transfiguration, the exact road and village for Emmaus is disputed, and the precise tomb location is not known with certainty. Those stops are labeled accordingly in their descriptions. Egypt and other regional pins are approximate study locations. See also the Church’s Bible Dictionary entry on Emmaus.
