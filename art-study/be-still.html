Be Still

Inspirational Art / Be Still

“Be still, and know that I am God” appears in a psalm filled with upheaval. Its stillness is not denial of trouble; it is trust in God in the middle of it.

Stillness in a Troubled World

Psalm 46 describes shaking earth, roaring waters, conflict, and nations in commotion. The invitation to be still comes inside that setting. Scriptural stillness can therefore mean releasing the demand to control everything and remembering that God remains God even when circumstances are unsettled.

Stillness Is Not Passivity

Trust does not prohibit responsible action. A disciple can pray, seek counsel, make decisions, obtain professional help when appropriate, and still cultivate an inward willingness to stop striving against what cannot be controlled. Faith joins action with surrender to God.
