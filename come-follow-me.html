From reading to living

Give one passage your full attention

Try this during any week of study, on your own or with someone else. Bring your scriptures and a notebook, and begin with the official lesson.

Read in context

What is happening here?

Read the verses around your chosen passage. Identify who is speaking, who is listening, and what question or situation the passage addresses. Write down what the text says before deciding what it means for you.

Notice and connect

What helps me look to Christ?

Choose a phrase that draws your attention. Follow a footnote or the lesson’s cross-reference. Compare the passages carefully, and leave room for a question you cannot yet answer.

Discuss and act

What will I carry into today?

Invite each person to share one observation without pressure to give the same answer. Choose a specific act of kindness, a prayer, or a conversation to follow your study. Return later and record what you noticed.

Ideas to Improve Learning at Home and at Church

A gentle weekly rhythm

Seven days of scripture and discipleship

Use the whole pattern or choose one step. The purpose is not to finish a checklist. It is to hear the Lord and follow Him.

1

Begin

Read the lesson introduction. Write one question you are carrying.

2

Read

Read the opening portion. Mark repeated names, promises, warnings, and invitations.

3

Trust

Look for Christ. Notice what the Lord does, promises, teaches, or asks.

4

Study more deeply

Use the official lesson. Follow one question or cross-reference that deepens understanding.

5

Study together

Study together. Choose one verse and make room for every person to share.

6

Act

Act. Practice one truth in prayer, relationships, worship, or service.

7

Remember

Share one truth about Christ, record one impression, and choose one action.

Study your way

One lesson, several faithful paths

Choose the setting that fits your life today. Every path returns to scripture and Jesus Christ.

Pause, pray, and respond

Let a passage stay with you

There is no need to hurry past a verse that deserves your attention. Choose one of these invitations, bring your scriptures, and give yourself a quiet moment to respond.

Begin with a prayer

Before reading, name the help you hope to receive. Afterward, write one thing the passage teaches about Jesus Christ and one question you would like to keep studying. Let an unanswered question remain part of tomorrow’s prayer.

Listen, then remember

Return to a phrase that drew your attention. Read the surrounding verses and record the thought or invitation you want to remember. If studying with others, invite each person to share or simply listen; a quiet response is welcome too.

Choose one act of discipleship

Consider how the passage could shape an ordinary moment today: your patience, an apology, a generous act, or time with someone who needs care. Choose one response you can carry out, then return to your notes later and reflect on what you learned.

Study these invitations alongside the official learning guidance.
