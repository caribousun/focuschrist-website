A gentle weekly rhythm

Seven days of scripture and discipleship

Use the whole pattern or choose one step. The purpose is not to finish a checklist. It is to hear the Lord and follow Him.

1

Begin

Read the lesson introduction. Write one question you are carrying.

2

Read

Read the opening portion. Mark repeated names, promises, warnings, and invitations.

3

Trust

Look for Christ. Notice what the Lord does, promises, teaches, or asks.

4

Speak

Use the official lesson. Follow one question or cross-reference that deepens understanding.

5

Become

Study together. Choose one verse and make room for every person to share.

6

Ponder

Act. Practice one truth in prayer, relationships, worship, or service.

7

Remember

Share one truth about Christ, record one impression, and choose one action.
