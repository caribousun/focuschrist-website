# Section opening wording review — 2026-09-20

Following the restoration of individual studies, Wyatt explicitly authorized a wording-only correction for clipped Continue pills, naming Eternal Marriage and Look Unto Me and asking for an audit of other section heroes. This is a narrow exception to the restored-section preservation rule, not a return to a sitewide hero redesign.

## Scope and treatment

The 27 non-main canonical openings were inspected at 390×740, 412×775, and 320×568. Ten overflowed at the two normal phone sizes: Eternal Marriage, Look Unto Me, Bible and Book of Mormon Together, Melchizedek Priesthood Restoration, Birth of Christ, Joseph Smith Likeness, and the four complete Art studies (Living Christ, Good Shepherd, Little Children, Be Still).

Only these ten receive reversible mobile wording changes. Redundant subtitles and long explanation move immediately below the opening; action labels are shortened where needed. Eternal Marriage uses a concise Christ-centered lead. Look Unto Me retains its exact scripture quotation, reference, explanation, and destination URLs. Its Continue pill enters that retained introduction before the main study. The mobile titles for Birth of Christ, Joseph Smith, and the Children art study are shorter; original titles return on desktop.

No stylesheet, artwork, hero dimensions, spacing, pill styling, or original study action URL is changed. Shared consumer cache references are refreshed. The other 17 non-main openings and all 13 main-page designs remain preserved.

## Verification

- 40 rendered checks across all ten repaired pages at 375×740, 390×740, 412×775, and 430×844 pass: full Continue pills and actions fit, labels do not clip, touch targets remain at least 44px, and there is no horizontal overflow.
- Ten desktop opening geometry/text/style comparisons match the original presentation. The 17 untouched section openings match at 390×740.
- All ten remain readable with unclipped controls at 200% text, allowing the opening to grow.
- Keyboard Continue on Look Unto Me displays the retained exact quotation and linked reference.
- Structural regression verifies exact desktop text and link restoration after mobile/desktop changes, unchanged outside-scope studies, accessible compact action labels, and valid retained-copy destinations.
- Independent review approved the wording changes and retained-copy navigation.

At 320×568, the preserved original image frames leave too little space for many complete introductions. Wording alone cannot guarantee first-screen fit at that height. These openings remain naturally scrollable; images and approved layouts were not resized to compensate. This limitation was communicated during work. Technical verification is not owner visual approval.
