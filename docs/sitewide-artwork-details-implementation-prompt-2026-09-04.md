# Site-Wide Artwork Detail Experience Implementation Prompt

## Objective

Extend the approved Mission artwork detail experience to every appropriate supporting artwork across focusChrist. When a visitor selects an applicable image, present useful context before offering the official study source and full-resolution image.

## Scope

- Add the detail experience to supporting artwork on Home, Ask, Answers, and Church History.
- Add it to the four Featured Art and Study cards at the bottom of the Art page.
- Preserve the existing Mission detail experience and its approved content.
- Preserve the Art gallery's existing full-screen browsing viewer.
- Preserve every hero, logo, icon, approved image file, image crop, caption, page section, and existing destination.
- Keep dedicated Art and Study pages as the full context destination. Their main artwork continues to open directly at full resolution because the surrounding page already explains the work.

## Content Standard

- Identify generated or interpretive scenes as symbolic artwork, contemporary portrayals, historical portrayals, or devotional artwork as appropriate.
- Never describe an image as a documentary photograph of an event unless verified.
- Use concise explanations grounded in the page's already reviewed content.
- Link factual and doctrinal explanations to existing official Church or scripture sources.
- Do not claim an artist, date, provenance, or historical detail that the reviewed source does not establish.
- Use ordinary punctuation. Do not introduce em dashes or en dashes.

## Interaction Standard

- Use a native accessible dialog that visually follows the approved Mission experience.
- Support mouse, touch, keyboard activation, Escape, backdrop closing, visible focus, focus return, and body scroll locking.
- Preserve each original anchor destination as the no-JavaScript fallback.
- Preserve modified clicks so visitors may open the original destination in a new tab.
- Show the selected image, title, two short explanatory paragraphs, an official source, a full-size image action, and Close.
- On Featured Art and Study cards, also show a direct action to open the complete study page.
- Keep the dialog inside the viewport and stack the image, text, and actions cleanly on narrow screens.

## Verification Standard

- Inventory all HTML image elements and document every intentional exclusion.
- Confirm no image files changed.
- Confirm all new triggers match one and only one reviewed content record.
- Confirm every referenced local image exists and every source URL is already approved or added to the content audit.
- Run JavaScript syntax checks, all relevant repository QA suites, static content audit, source integrity checks, and whitespace validation.
- Obtain an independent review before publication.
- Publish only after all checks pass, then verify the live HTML, JavaScript, and CSS directly.
