# Approved priesthood artwork replacement

Owner approved the three final Joseph-reference revisions in this conversation. Scope is the existing history-page lead and its two companion pictures; the proposed Kirtland section was canceled.

Joseph uses the actual owner-approved portrait as identity reference, interpreted at age 23. Peter was revised at owner direction to brown hair and beard and a comparable adult apparent age to James and John. The three apostles retain distinct natural clothing across the set: Peter ivory/flax; James stone-gray/cream; John oatmeal/taupe. Their exact ages, faces and clothing colors at the visitation are not established by the cited account and are artistic interpretations. No assertion of a precise Melchizedek restoration date or recipient order is introduced.
