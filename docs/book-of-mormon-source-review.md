# Book of Mormon enrichment: independent source review

Reviewed September 19, 2026. Scope: the 22-stop story journey and 39 selected new caption sets in the Book of Mormon study. Source review passes; this record does not certify final pixels, rendered behavior, deployment, or owner acceptance. No HTML was edited during this review.

## Final additions

- Captain Moroni: [Alma 46:12–21](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/46?lang=eng&id=p12-p21#p12) supports the torn coat inscription, pole, protective armor, prayer, and subsequent appeal among the people. Captain Moroni is distinct from Mormon's son Moroni. Exact garment/armor materials and reconstructed script are artistic choices, not authenticated artifacts.
- Helaman and the young warriors: [Alma 56:44–48](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/56?lang=eng&id=p44-p48#p44) supports the father/sons language, youthful soldiers, courage, and remembrance of their mothers. [Alma 53:16–22](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/53?lang=eng&id=p16-p22#p16) identifies the two thousand and their choice of Helaman. Visual ages 14–18 follow Wyatt's direction; the text gives no numerical ages. Helaman's apparent age is also an artistic interpretation. [Alma 57:21–27](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/57?lang=eng&id=p21-p27#p21) distinguishes survival from freedom from injury: all were wounded. This commander is Helaman son of Alma, not his son Helaman who fathered Nephi and Lehi.
- Mormon's final appeal: [Mormon 7:1–10](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/morm/7?lang=eng&id=p1-p10#p1) supports repentance, laying down weapons, belief in Christ, resurrection, baptism, and the complementary witnesses of the records. The illustrated writing posture and woodland location are interpretation. An elderly appearance is appropriate; a precise age is not asserted in visitor copy.
- Moroni and Joseph: [Joseph Smith—History 1:59](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/js-h/1?lang=eng&id=p59#p59) specifies September 22, 1827 and the messenger's charge. [Verse 3](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/js-h/1?lang=eng&id=p3#p3) gives December 23, 1805 as Joseph's birth date, establishing age 21. [Verses 51–54](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/js-h/1?lang=eng&id=p51-p54#p51) distinguish his earlier discovery and annual instruction from receiving the plates. The cloth wrapping, hand positions, nighttime lighting, and arrangement are artistic interpretation, not details established by verse 59. The caption explicitly frames the scene as imagined. Joseph's owner-mandated reference identity remains controlling, adapted to the documented age.

## Earlier reviewed source boundaries

- Ether 3 portrays premortal Christ in His spirit body, not resurrected Christ. No crucifixion marks belong to that scene.
- 1 Nephi 11 healing is the mortal ministry Nephi sees in vision, not a physical visit to Nephi's camp.
- Enos hears the Lord's assurance; Alma's conversion includes an angel and an inward plea to Jesus. Neither scene invents a bodily Christ appearance.
- Alma the Elder, Alma the Younger, the two Helamans, the successive Nephis, Captain Moroni, Mormon, and his son Moroni remain distinct.
- Abish already believed before the household event (Alma 19:16). Her artwork source now includes verses 16–30. The Alma/Amulek source includes Alma 32:1–28 for its seed/desire language. The Bountiful healing source includes 3 Nephi 17:5–9 for the tears preceding the invitation.
- 3 Nephi 11:1–17 supports the temple gathering, white-robed descent, introduction, individual witness, and collective worship. Five separate compositions develop that sequence; they do not imply five descents.
- The lilies instruction in 3 Nephi 13:25–34 specifically addresses the twelve. 3 Nephi 17 distinguishes prayer among children, individual blessings, and subsequent angelic ministry with encircling fire. The unnamed angels' particular ages, faces, and genders are artistic choices; none is identified as a particular deceased person.
- 3 Nephi 19:19–23 supports Christ praying a little apart. 3 Nephi 23:6–13 concerns the omitted fulfillment of Samuel's prophecy, not Samuel physically attending Christ. Chapters 27–28 support the Church-name teaching and the three disciples' desire to continue ministering.
- Ether 12:23–27 and 39 are Moroni's personal testimony within his abridgment, not Ether's first-person account.

No caption establishes that Book of Mormon events occurred at a known archaeological site or that a Church geography has been authenticated.
