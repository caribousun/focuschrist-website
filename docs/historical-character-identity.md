Use the [Joseph Smith Papers photographic reference](https://www.josephsmithpapers.org/person/orrin-porter-rockwell): Edward Martin photograph, circa 1866, Church History Library. Exact image URL: https://www.josephsmithpapers.org/bc-jsp/content/jsp/images/content/library/images/A1-Page-640a-Rockwell-Orrin-Porter-PH-9938-obverse.jpg . The [Church History Library reference article](https://churchhistorylibrary.churchofjesuschrist.org/blog/ask-us-top-five-reference-questions-about-orrin-porter-rockwell?lang=eng) also shows an undated image labeled PH 5962; it appears to repeat the same photographic composition and must not be counted as independent corroboration.

The current JSP biography gives June 1814 as his birth date, citing Council of Fifty and Seventies records; the Church History Library article gives June 25, 1813. Preserve this discrepancy: use approximately 29–30 for Christmas 1843, rather than a falsely exact age. The photograph is approximately 51–53 years old and requires careful age interpretation for that earlier scene. Do not automatically import its later receding hairline or mature beard into young depictions.

The [contemporary Christmas 1843 journal entry](https://www.josephsmithpapers.org/paper-summary/journal-december-1842-june-1844-book-3-15-july-1843-29-february-1844/218) documents hair falling over his shoulders, Joseph's recognition and joy, and his return from imprisonment. It supports a reunion scene. An embrace, clothing, exact beard shape, room furnishings, hair pigmentation, eye color and stature are not established by that entry and remain interpretation unless further reliable evidence is obtained. The monochrome photograph does not establish color. Keep Porter distinct from Joseph's locked identity.

Do not depict the hair-protection legend as proven invulnerability or promote berry-fundraising stories without verifying their source chain. The grand jury did not indict Porter for the attempted murder of Lilburn Boggs; this procedural result does not settle all historical questions. His Church History study connects friendship and early Restoration community, not a claim that he was a Book of Mormon translator or one of its formal plate witnesses.
