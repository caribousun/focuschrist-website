## Doctrinal and historical boundaries

- Resurrection and immortality are promised to all through Christ; eternal life/exaltation is a distinct gift through Him and faithful covenant discipleship.
- The spirit world is an intermediate state. Do not confuse Alma40's temporary darkness with the final outer darkness of sons of perdition.
- The Fall opens mortal experience; Christ redeems. Do not blame Eve alone or teach original guilt of children.
- Agency does not mean a victim chose or deserved harm. Race, disability or social circumstances do not measure premortal worth.
- D&C138 distinguishes Christ's ministry among righteous spirits from the messengers He authorizes to teach others. Proxy ordinances preserve choice.
- Degrees of glory are not planets or stages of mortality. Sun/moon/stars are comparisons, not astronomical addresses.
- D&C137 preserves God's knowledge of works, desires and opportunities; do not assign a person's final kingdom.
- Family promises must not become guarantees concerning an unwilling person or condemnation of present family circumstances.
- John17 does not specify every physical detail. Third Nephi26 withholds the children's words. No invented dialogue.
- Paul at Athens is an existing-owner reference preview linked to the God study, not a new composition. Joseph F. Smith pondering is supported by D&C138:1–6; his exact furnishings still require careful staging.
- Keep art-production notes internal. Visitor captions should describe accepted pixels and lead naturally into scripture, while retaining material source boundaries.
