## Evidence and interpretive limits

- Eden is sacred-space theology grounded in the Church's temple introduction and Moses 5, not proof of a built temple in Eden. The picture contains no invented divine appearance or named figures.
- Abraham's altars in Genesis 12 precede monumental temples. Genesis dates his departure from Haran at 75; an unpeopled altar/tent avoids unresolved ancient likenesses.
- Exodus 25–27 and 40 describe the tabernacle. It is a portable sanctuary, not a stone temple. Exact reconstruction is uncertain; basic scriptural furnishings and enclosure should remain coherent.
- Solomon's temple, the postexilic temple, and Herod's expansion need distinct labels. Avoid the old Talmage article's precise completion chronology, which is dated, and its sweeping polemical descriptions of other religious buildings. Use its supported high-level succession only; 1 Kings 8 and Ezra 3 anchor the actual study.
- Nephi's temple is testimony in the Book of Mormon. 2 Nephi 5 supplies no location in a modern country, excavated footprint, or identified surviving pyramid. Any picture is an interpretive ancient sanctuary.
- Luke 2 explicitly gives Jesus' age as twelve. Adult Christ identity must be aged convincingly, never copied with beard onto a child. Adult ministry scenes have an approximate early-thirties age; exact day/year not asserted.
- Matthew 21 cleansing and healing are distinct actions. Do not import John's cord scourge into Matthew's account. No ethnic vilification. Mark 12's widow need not be elderly; her precise age is unknown.
- Bountiful artwork focuses on covenant instruction in 3 Nephi 20, not another generic arrival/children/healing image. No invented local geography or ceremony. Post-Resurrection hand/wrist marks must follow owner mandate wherever visible.
- Acts 3 depicts Peter and John acting in Christ's name, not an apparition of Christ. Named ancient identities must be checked against registry before generation.
- Kirtland chapter records D&C 110 as Joseph and Oliver's vision account. At root direction, illustration is architectural pulpits only: no recreated Christ apparition. Joseph was 30 and Oliver 29 if later authorized as visible people in an 1836 scene; do not include them without identity references.
- Original Nauvoo architecture, April 1877 St. George tower, and current Laie landscaping cannot be guessed from modern general memory. Obtain official visual references. Prefer explicitly contemporary views where that avoids false period architecture.
- Modern priesthood/temple racial restriction and its 1978 end are included using Official Declaration 2 and the Church History topic, without invented explanations for the restriction.
- Present-day chronology uses the dated August 20, 2026 Church Newsroom announcement of Salt Lake's April 5–October 1, 2027 celebration. This is a scheduled future event, not a completed opening. Do not insert a changing worldwide temple count.
