# Distinct later-ministry scenes in 3 Nephi

Researched 2026-09-19 against the current official Church scripture text, chapters 20–28, with the earlier-ministry coverage below. This inventory proposes additions; it does not replace the five retained scenes, technical review pending where noted. Owner appreciation and the request to retain those scenes do not certify later technical repairs. Composition suggestions are artistic interpretation, not additional historical claims. Exact clothing, architecture, chronology within an action, facial appearance, and camera positions are not supplied by these passages.

## Three strongest additions

### 1. Nephi brings the record to Christ

**Scripture:** [3 Nephi 23:6–14](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/3-ne/23?id=p6-p14&lang=eng#p6).

**Recorded action:** Jesus asks **Nephi** to bring the record. Nephi places it before Him. Jesus looks at the records and asks why the fulfillment of Samuel the Lamanite's prophecy—the saints rising and ministering—has not been written. The disciples affirm its fulfillment; Nephi remembers the omission; the account is added.

**Distinct visual:** An intimate, oblique view of Christ and Nephi with the physical record prominent between them, a few disciples witnessing. Capture the conversational exchange after Christ looks at the record: Christ addressing Nephi, Nephi looking back attentively. This creates meaningful interpersonal eye contact instead of another front-facing crowd scene.

**Do not invent:** Samuel physically present; Christ writing the record; legible fabricated glyphs; a modern bound book; exact record dimensions/material or writing implement; seated posture as a scriptural fact. Record form and location remain interpretive. Nephi is not Nephi the son of Lehi. Eye contact is a natural composition choice, not a quotation from the account.

### 2. Christ gives bread to His disciples

**Scripture:** [3 Nephi 20:1–9](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/3-ne/20?id=p1-p9&lang=eng#p1).

**Recorded action:** Christ tells the disciples and multitude to stop vocal prayer, continue praying in their hearts, and stand. He breaks and blesses bread, gives it to the disciples, and directs them to distribute bread and wine. Neither the disciples nor the multitude had brought bread or wine. The people receive the Spirit and glorify Him.

**Distinct visual:** A close side view of Christ extending broken bread to a standing disciple; another disciple turns to serve people in the background. The recipient's eyes meet Christ's, with hands and bread forming the center. Emphasize the exchange and service rather than a posed assembly. No individual recipient is named in these verses.

**Do not invent:** Fish, a picnic feast, loaves supplied by a child, baskets miraculously filling, modern sacrament trays, or a staged Last Supper table. The source does not describe how the bread and wine appeared. It specifically has the people stand; do not depict this chapter as a seated hillside feeding.

### 3. Christ turns toward the three disciples

**Scripture:** [3 Nephi 28:1–12](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/3-ne/28?id=p1-p12&lang=eng#p1); [28:25](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/3-ne/28?id=p25&lang=eng#p25).

**Recorded action:** Jesus asks the disciples individually about their desires. Nine ask to return to Him after their ministry. He turns toward the remaining three, who hesitate to speak, and acknowledges their desire to continue bringing souls to Him.

**Distinct visual:** A horizontal, small-group conversation: Christ in three-quarter profile turned toward three men, two looking directly at Him and one listening nearby. The nine may remain softly in the background. His turn toward these three is explicit; reciprocal gaze and exact hand gestures are compositional interpretation. Use a quiet, intent encounter instead of another kneeling multitude.

**Do not invent:** Names for the three; a priesthood ordination gesture or touch upon their heads. Verse 12 expressly says Christ touches the others with His finger **except the three**. Do not mix their later heavenly experience into this conversation or show its unrecorded content.

## Other scenes evaluated

- **An unnamed man restored to life:** [3 Nephi 26:15](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/3-ne/26?id=p15&lang=eng#p15) states that Christ raised a man from the dead. This is visually distinct and relevant, but supplies no name, posture, cause of death, surrounding relatives, location, words, or method. A painting would require substantially more interpretation. Do not import Lazarus, a tomb, a bier, or a specific family relationship into this Nephite event.
- **Children speak to their fathers:** [3 Nephi 26:14–16](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/3-ne/26?id=p14-p16&lang=eng#p14). Children speak after Christ ministers to them. Verse 16's next-day gathering follows His departure, so it must not automatically show Christ physically present. Their words are not recorded and must not be invented. The direct ministry of verse 14 is possible but resembles the existing children image.
- **Christ returns to the praying disciples:** [3 Nephi 27:1–7](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/3-ne/27?id=p1-p7&lang=eng#p1). While the disciples gather in prayer and fasting, Christ stands among them and answers their question about the Church's name. A smaller circle is supported, but resembles the chosen chapter 28 encounter; not a first-choice addition.

## Coverage of chapters 20–28

- [20](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/3-ne/20?lang=eng): bread/wine administration followed by covenant and gathering teachings.
- [21](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/3-ne/21?lang=eng): a future sign, repentance, gathering, and New Jerusalem. Prophetic future events are not events enacted before the gathered Nephites.
- [22](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/3-ne/22?lang=eng): Isaiah's imagery and covenant reassurance. A tent, gemstones, or a smith here would illustrate teaching, not document Christ visiting those objects.
- [23](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/3-ne/23?lang=eng): strongest distinctive physical action is Nephi bringing the record.
- [24](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/3-ne/24?lang=eng) and [25](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/3-ne/25?lang=eng): Christ gives Malachi's words for writing and explains them. A refinery, tithing storehouse, or Elijah appearing beside Christ would wrongly literalize the quoted teaching as an event in this visit.
- [26](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/3-ne/26?lang=eng): ministry summary, children, healing, raising a man, repeated visits; many teachings deliberately omitted.
- [27](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/3-ne/27?lang=eng): disciples' prayer, Christ's presence, His Church and gospel.
- [28](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/3-ne/28?lang=eng): individual desires, three disciples' continuing service, and later experiences. Prefer the explicitly narrated conversation over speculative heavenly imagery.

## Eye contact and compositional review

For all additions, test whether Christ's eyes and the recipient's eyes point toward each other at believable angles. The record scene may first direct both sets of eyes at the record; use the subsequent spoken question if mutual gaze is desired. Avoid every bystander looking at the viewer. Natural eye contact does not require every face to be visible. Keep source-grounded gestures ahead of decorative symmetry.

## Earlier ministry coverage: chapters 11–19

- [3 Nephi 11:1, 8, 14–21](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/3-ne/11?lang=eng): the setting is the temple in Bountiful; Christ descends in a white robe; people approach individually to witness His wounds; Nephi is then called forward and receives authority to baptize. Do not merge this named encounter into an arbitrary unnamed group.
- [Chapters 12–16](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/3-ne/12?lang=eng): principally teachings, including Beatitudes, prayer, the law, and other sheep. Images within sayings are not additional events enacted before the people. Chapter 16's account of other sheep does not supply a new specific local scene or a geographic identification.
- [3 Nephi 17:5–24](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/3-ne/17?lang=eng): compassion, healing, children gathered around Christ, the multitude kneeling, Christ kneeling in prayer, blessings for individual children, then angels. The prayer's marvelous words are not supplied; do not write invented dialogue. Distinguish prayer from the later individual blessings.
- [3 Nephi 18:1–11](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/3-ne/18?id=p1-p11&lang=eng#p1): disciples fetch bread and wine, and the multitude sits on the ground. This differs from chapter 20, where Christ first commands the people to stand and no one has brought bread or wine. Chapter 18:7, 11 explicitly teaches remembrance.
- [3 Nephi 19:10–15](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/3-ne/19?id=p10-p15&lang=eng#p10): Nephi and the disciples' baptisms occur before Christ returns among them. Do not place Jesus physically beside those baptisms as if the sequence said He was present. Later prayer and His direct interaction with the disciples are separate moments.
