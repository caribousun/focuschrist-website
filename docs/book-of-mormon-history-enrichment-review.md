## Historical and visual review

The exact owner-approved Joseph identity and Home Christ identity remain authoritative. Hyrum's own death mask controls his new facial reconstruction. Oliver, David, Emma, Martin, Porter and Parley were researched from historical records and reliable likeness sources and adapted to event ages. Ancient messengers and Mary Whitmer have no authenticated photographic likeness; their depictions are interpretations.

Later witness recollections are distinguished from contemporary records. The field study distinguishes plowing from Lucy Mack Smith's separate harrowing and plaster-spreading account. Richmond and Liberty are separate settings. The Liberty reconstruction uses timber lining, narrow windows and a low ceiling without the inaccurate claim that all prisoners could never stand. The Melchizedek restoration has no asserted exact day. Kirtland's six tall side windows per tier and five dormers were checked against the reference; an erroneous five-window candidate was rejected. The original Nauvoo Temple uses a horizontal angel vane rather than the modern upright statue.

Every selected image received full-size and phone review for identity, photographic clarity, anatomy, setting, scene-appropriate expression and coherent gaze. Rejected plow, gaze and architectural candidates were kept out of page assets. David's final plow has coherent period construction, soil contact and a parked position. Generated illustrations are not evidence that the illustrated events occurred or photographs of original manuscripts/artifacts.

Scholarship is presented with its limitations: textual reconstruction does not independently prove supernatural causation; chiasmus and stylometry require controls and do not settle authorship alone; archaeological proposals and population genetics are not overstated. Faith and spiritual testimony remain distinct from empirical demonstration.
