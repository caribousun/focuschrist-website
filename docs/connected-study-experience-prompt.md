# focusChrist connected study experience: governing execution prompt

## Objective
Build and maintain a reverent, welcoming, source-grounded study experience across the whole focusChrist website. A visitor should be able to spend meaningful time understanding a subject, read scripture in context, reflect honestly, and continue into a related page without losing the thread. A short answer followed by a list of links is an introduction, not a completed study.

## Read and inspect before changing
Load the Master Brain and relevant Focus controls, current state, repository instructions, published pages and existing assets. Audit Home, Ask, Answers and every answer article, Art and every art study, Mission, History, Pioneers, Watch and About. Inspect visible sections, cards, artwork details, source links, related studies and return paths. Respect each page's purpose. Utility/error pages need clear recovery, not an unrelated devotional essay.

## The study standard
For each subject, provide a clear opening answer, meaningful explanation, relevant scripture with exact passage references and accessible source links, the context needed to understand those passages, and important questions or distinctions a reader is likely to have. Explain what each selected resource contributes. Offer reflection and practical application as invitations, clearly distinguished from scripture or official teaching. Give readers useful places to pause and resume with descriptive headings and in-page navigation where length warrants it.

Depth is measured by learning value, not word count, number of links or repeated sections. Do not pad strong pages or impose the same outline on every subject. Explain unfamiliar terms. Develop the particular question rather than appending generic encouragement. Where traditions disagree, distinguish self-identification, doctrine and historical interpretation; represent other traditions through their own primary sources. State uncertainty honestly.

## Care and voice
Write like a thoughtful person who respects the reader. Use clear, warm, specific language. Make room for doubt, grief, differing backgrounds and family circumstances. Avoid shame, pressure, canned reassurance, robotic transitions, repetitive formulas, long dashes and claims to know God's purpose for an individual's suffering. Do not promise an emotional response, healing timetable, spiritual certainty or personal outcome. Preserve agency and appropriate professional support. Faith is not measured by how quickly someone feels better.

## Connected learning
Every meaningful transition must explain why the destination is relevant and what the visitor can explore there. Give study pages a few intentional next steps, not an unrelated link collection. Connect artwork to its scripture and full study; connect video topics to study; connect historical questions to evidence and context; connect broad introductions to specific questions. Use normal crawlable links and verified section anchors. Preserve full-size image behavior, direct source fallbacks, keyboard access and return navigation. Do not silently rewrite a visitor's question or treat page context as a new AI answer.

## Source integrity
Use scripture, official Church resources and primary historical records. Use a tradition's own primary source when explaining its beliefs. Verify new factual assertions, quotations, identities, dates, verse ranges and destinations. Prefer brief original explanation to copied passages; invite visitors to read the original. Distinguish scripture, official teaching, history, interpretation, original reflection and symbolic artwork. Update the existing content-audit ledger only after real semantic review; hashes detect changes and do not prove truth.

## Preserve and implement
Preserve approved artwork, exact likenesses, heroes, brand, working layouts, AI safeguards and existing useful content. Make additive, coherent improvements using the established visual language. Avoid generating new artwork or changing providers, spending, accounts or doctrine without authorization. The owner's request authorizes this content and continuity improvement; unrelated work remains outside scope.

## Acceptance and closure
Review the actual destination pages, not just the landing page. Check the richer passages and their sources, meaningful next steps, internal paths and anchors, source destinations, artwork study/full-size interactions, readability and responsive behavior. Run required repository gates and independent review before commit. Publish through the authorized release path and verify the deployed content and representative user journeys. Record precisely what was verified and any limitation; do not claim universal perfection. Preserve implementation, evidence and continuation in Focus; promote only reusable learning to Master. Future additions inherit this standard and must not regress into shallow summaries with bare links.

## Execute now
Apply this standard across the current site. Use “Are Latter-day Saints Christian?” as a detailed acceptance example, strengthen the other answer and art studies according to their gaps, and improve thematic transitions on the main pages. Complete source review, independent verification, release and live acceptance before reporting completion.
