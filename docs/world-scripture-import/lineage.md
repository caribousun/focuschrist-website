# Existing World and Scripture timeline media

This delivery imports existing bytes from the supplied World and Scripture timeline. It does not claim new artwork generation or retroactive precreation review. The selected hero is the existing hero Wyatt explicitly asked to retain. All 193 source photographs retain their Commons file pages, retrieved creator and license metadata, and individual credits. Three tiny vendor images are Leaflet 1.9.4 and MapLibre 5.6.0 runtime utilities, not study artwork.

The preserved English-map source archive has SHA256 `034a982736431a818956851a00b0dcd568c84707f435b826cc7065e43690bc1b`. The published image bytes were extracted without recompression or pixel editing. A reversible mapping restores every image URL to its prior embedded data URI. The original DATA records, ten route definitions and world outline are otherwise unchanged. The unused old raster-map tile data is retained in that archive and excluded from this online English-map delivery.

Source photo metadata was retrieved from the Wikimedia Commons imageinfo API for all 193 linked file pages. Creator names are preserved where supplied; absent creators are not invented. The Free Art License photograph explicitly credits A.Savin, Wikipedia. The GFDL 1.2 photograph is distributed with the complete license text. The individual versioned Creative Commons licenses and source credits remain separate; a general Commons link does not replace them. Display copies may be smaller or differently encoded than the linked original source files.

The photographs are separately accessible under their respective licenses; their inclusion does not assert endorsement by their creators or institutions. Vendor license texts and bundled notices are included intact. The timeline header artwork selection and existing media import do not waive any governing requirements for future newly generated artwork.

Technical hashes establish byte and review-record consistency. They do not independently authenticate ownership, judge artwork, or establish future compliance. Actual source and finished-view review remains separately attributed.
