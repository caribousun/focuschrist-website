# focusChrist Supporting Artwork Roadmap

Updated: 2026-09-03

## Governing reference

The Missionary page is the composition reference. Supporting art belongs inside the reading rhythm through editorial splits, alternating visual weight, generous spacing, and restrained surfaces. Heroes remain unchanged. Every new candidate requires page-specific approval before integration.

All candidates must use cinematic photographic realism, natural anatomy and fabric, reverent light, the approved focusChrist likeness of Jesus Christ when He appears, and no cartoon, painted, glossy, fantasy, or game-rendered treatment.

## Performance release gate

Each approved artwork receives responsive WebP variants sized for its rendered placement. Supporting images include explicit dimensions, `loading="lazy"`, and `decoding="async"`. A delivered variant should remain at or below 120 KB when practical and must be visually inspected at desktop and mobile crop sizes before release.
